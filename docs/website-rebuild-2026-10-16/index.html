3 · What we say

One sentence, and the words that are banned

Today, as a visitor reads it

"A done-for-you cold outbound system that books calls."

Hero "We Install Cold-to-Sold, live in 48 hours". A category every founder gets pitched weekly.

New

"We run the outbound experiments your team does not have the hands for, across email, LinkedIn, mail and phone, and you see the replies before you pay."

For proven B2B products. Method: the Pass System. Front door: a qualified trial.

Who it is for

B2B products that already sell: paying customers, a sales conversation before the deal, a few thousand reachable buyer companies. Often a founder or a 1 to 3 person sales team.

What a tool will not do for them

  • Find buyers by signal: companies hiring now for the seat the product replaces, plus the person who signs
  • Run one market from many sender identities, so ten angles get tested on one list
  • 25 days across email, LinkedIn, mail and phone, stopping the moment someone replies
  • A reply agent and follow-up so no warm thread dies
  • Run before they pay

Never in the pitch

  • Cold email agency, outbound agency, lead gen, B2B outbound (hero, nav, homepage headings, /pricing, /trial; allowed for SEO in the blog, meta, FAQ answers)
  • "We Install Cold-to-Sold"
  • Email volume as the reason to pick a plan
  • "Your team follows up by call and SMS"
  • Prices for performance models
  • The word "free" as a plan name
  • Em dashes or en dashes
