Frequently asked questions

What is an ICP in B2B sales?

ICP stands for Ideal Customer Profile — a precise description of the companies most likely to buy from you. It includes industry, company size, revenue range, geography, tech stack, and decision-maker titles. A tight ICP is the single biggest lever in cold outbound.

How do I use this in Apollo?

The tool outputs exact Apollo filter values — industry names match Apollo's dropdown options, headcount ranges match Apollo's sliders, and job titles are formatted for Apollo's search. Copy and paste directly into your Apollo filter panel.

What are buying signals?

Buying signals are real-world events that suggest a company needs your solution right now — a new hire, a funding round, a job posting for a tool you replace. Emails sent around signals get 3-5x higher reply rates than generic outreach.
