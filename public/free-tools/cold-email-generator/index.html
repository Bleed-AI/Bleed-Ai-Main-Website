Frequently asked questions

What makes a cold email get replies?

Signal-based emails. A buying signal is a real event at the prospect's company that makes your offer relevant right now — a new hire in a role you help, a funding round (budget to spend), a job posting for a tool you replace. Emails written around signals get 3-5x higher reply rates than generic templates.

How long should a cold email be?

Under 100 words. Three to five sentences: show you know something specific about them, what you do, what's in it for them, and a low-friction CTA. Long emails signal mass outreach. Short and specific emails feel personal — and personal emails get replies.

What's the best cold email subject line?

3-5 words, lowercase, specific to the recipient. "saw your Series A", "re: your SDR hire", "quick question, Acme Corp". Boring and specific outperforms clever every time. The subject line's only job is to get the email opened.

What reply rate should I expect?

Baseline with no personalization: 1-3% positive reply rate. One relevant signal: 8-15%. Stacked signals with a tight ICP: 25-40% in the right niches. Most campaigns fail at the targeting layer, not the copy layer.
