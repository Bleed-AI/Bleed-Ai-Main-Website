Frequently asked questions

How many domains do I need for cold email?

The rule is 1 domain per 2 inboxes, each sending a max of 30-40 emails/day. For 1,000 emails/month you need 2-3 domains. For 10,000 emails/month you need 15-20. This calculator gives you the exact number for your volume.

Why can't I just use my main domain?

Cold email triggers spam complaints. When spam complaints hit your domain, email providers (Google, Microsoft) flag it. Once blacklisted, every email from that domain — including your sales emails, invoices, and client communication — goes to spam. Separate sending domains keep your primary domain clean.

How long does warmup take?

3-4 weeks minimum for a new domain to be ready to send at volume. A warmup tool (Instantly, Smartlead) sends real emails in your inbox and gets replies, building sender reputation with Gmail and Outlook before you start your campaign.

Where should I buy sending domains?

Namecheap for simplicity and price (~$10-12/year per .com). Cloudflare Registrar for cheapest cost but more technical setup. Avoid registering sending domains with the same registrar as your primary domain — keeps things clean if you need to rotate.
