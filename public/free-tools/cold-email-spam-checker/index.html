Frequently asked questions

Why is my cold email going to spam?

The most common causes: spam trigger words in the subject or body, emails that look AI-generated, missing DNS records (SPF/DKIM/DMARC), sending from an unwarmed domain, and copy that reads like a mass blast. This tool checks all of these.

What's a good spam score?

80+ is solid. 90+ is excellent. Most first-time audits come back between 45 and 65 — meaning emails are landing in spam and the sender has no idea. Every point above 80 measurably improves inbox placement.

Does this check my actual domain reputation?

This tool audits your email content and setup checklist. For live domain reputation, use Google Postmaster Tools or MXToolbox after running this audit.
